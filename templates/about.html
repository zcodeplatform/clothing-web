Our Story

Clothes made for women who move through the world with intention.

Eclothing began as a small studio with one sewing machine and a simple idea: everyday clothes should feel as considered as occasion wear. Ten years later, that idea still guides every collection we design.

We design in small runs, work closely with the workshops that make our pieces, and listen carefully to the women who wear them. The result is a wardrobe of tailoring, soft essentials and accessories that work together, season after season.
